# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People logging strength training during and between gym sessions. They need fast, reliable set logging and an honest view of their progress.

## Product Purpose

LFT is a local-first workout tracker for creating routines, recording sessions, reviewing history, and understanding training progress.

## Operating Context

The product is primarily used one-handed on a phone in a gym, including with limited or no connectivity. Desktop remains useful for reviewing history and settings.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing authentication, local storage, Supabase synchronization, templates, active sessions, timers, unit preferences, exports, and historical records are product truth and must be preserved. Stored fitness data must never be reset as part of presentation work.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is LFT. The supplied implementation brief makes a premium, focused, Apple Fitness-inspired training companion the required direction, with genuine dark and light themes.

## Evidence on Hand

The existing product source, local data model, and `C:/Users/Jugal/Downloads/Fitness App Design System Showcase.png` are the authoritative implementation evidence.

## Product Principles

- Starting and logging a workout stays faster than browsing analytics.
- Metrics and rewards derive from actual recorded sessions.
- Offline-first behavior is more important than visual novelty.
- Important controls remain legible and reachable on a phone.
