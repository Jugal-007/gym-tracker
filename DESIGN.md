# LFT Design System

## Direction

An athletic instrument panel: calm, dense enough for training, and deliberately tactile without skeuomorphism. It takes the reference image's layered dark surfaces, blue performance accent, compact metrics, rounded training controls, and clean light counterpart as the visual target.

## Foundations

- Deep graphite and navy-black backgrounds in dark mode; soft cloud-white surfaces in light mode.
- Electric blue is the action color. Emerald communicates completion, amber communicates rewards, and rose communicates destructive actions.
- Outfit supplies a clear athletic display voice; tabular numerals keep weights and timers stable.
- Cards use 18–24px radii, subtle elevation, and quiet borders. Gradients are reserved for the home hero and journey status.

## Interaction

- Primary actions are full-width, high-contrast controls.
- Mobile navigation contains Home, Progress, Workout, History, and Profile only.
- Active training removes global navigation and keeps rest controls immediate.
- Motion is brief and respects reduced-motion preferences.
